08 Mathematical Interpretation

Concise viva explanation.

The image is treated as a matrix A. Rank(A) describes independent structure in that matrix. The eigenvectors of ATA give orthogonal directions in the image vector space. Selecting k dominant eigenvectors gives a lower-dimensional representation. Projection calculates P = AVk, reconstruction calculates Ak = PVkT, and the Frobenius error measures how much information is lost.

For this upload: k = {{ result.selected_k }}, rank(A) = {{ result.rank }}, relative error = {{ result.relative_error|floatformat:4 }}%.
